Data Deletion Request

Last updated: June 1, 2026

You have the right to request deletion of your Credibility Radar account and all personal data we hold about you. This page explains exactly how to do that and what to expect.

How to request deletion

1
Send an email to the address below from the email address associated with your Credibility Radar account.
2
Use the subject line: "Delete my account"
3
Optionally include any specific data you want removed (e.g. "delete all my analysis history only").
privacy@credibilityradar.app

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (email, password hash, profile data)
  • All analyses you have submitted (text inputs, scores, history)
  • Authentication tokens and session data
  • Diagnostic and crash logs associated with your user ID

What we retain (and why)

  • Subscription / payment records: retained by Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat for tax and accounting purposes. We do not control these records — contact Apple or Google directly to request removal.
  • Anonymized aggregate analytics: non-identifiable usage counts may be retained for product analytics.
  • Legal compliance records: if required by law, we may retain limited records (e.g. fraud prevention) for the legally required period.

Timeline

We will confirm receipt of your request within 72 hours and complete deletion within 30 days. You will receive an email confirmation once deletion is complete.

Verification

To protect your account, we will only process deletion requests sent from the email address registered to the account. If you no longer have access to that email, include enough information for us to verify your identity (approximate sign-up date, last analysis topic, etc.).

Questions

For questions about this process or our data practices, see our Privacy Policy or contact support@credibilityradar.app.
