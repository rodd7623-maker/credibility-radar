## Upload Instructions

### Apple App Store (App Store Connect)
1. Go to App Store Connect → Your App → App Store tab
2. Under "iPhone Screenshots" select 6.9" Display (iPhone 15 Pro Max)
3. Upload `01–05` PNG files in order
4. Also upload under 6.5" and 5.5" display sizes (App Store Connect will scale)

### Google Play Store (Play Console)
1. Go to Play Console → Store Listing → Graphics
2. Under "Phone screenshots" upload `01–05`
3. Under "Feature graphic" upload `feature-graphic-1024x500.png`
