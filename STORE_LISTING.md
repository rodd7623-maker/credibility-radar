## 📝 Full Description (Play Store, 4000 char max)

```
Credibility Radar uses AI to expose the gap between real expertise and confident-sounding fluff. Paste any text — a pitch deck, a candidate's answer, a consultant's email, a viral tweet — and get an instant Depth of Knowledge (DoK) score with a clear breakdown of why.

WHAT IT MEASURES
Four signals, scored independently and combined into one number 0–100:

• Technical Specificity — Does the speaker name concrete tools, units, mechanisms? Or only vague abstractions?
• Structural Anchoring — Is the argument grounded in causes, sequences, and constraints? Or does it float?
• Camouflage Detection — How much corporate jargon, hedging, and buzzword soup masks the actual claim?
• Logical Coherence — Do the parts connect, or do contradictions appear when you read closely?

WHO IT'S FOR
• Hiring managers screening candidate responses
• Investors and analysts vetting pitch decks and founder calls
• Journalists fact-checking press releases
• Researchers cutting through review-paper hype
• Anyone tired of being talked at by people who sound smart but say nothing

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
Credibility Radar isn't a fact checker — it's a depth checker. A claim can be technically true and still be hollow. Our AI looks at how something is said, not just what is said, and tells you whether the speaker is anchored in real understanding.

FEATURES
✓ Text and voice input — paste a transcript or speak into the mic
✓ Color-coded score gauge with four-metric breakdown
✓ Highlighted fluff phrases pulled directly from your input
✓ Full analysis history (Pro)
✓ Side-by-side strengths and weaknesses
✓ Sample analyses to calibrate your intuition

FREE & PRO
• Free: 3 scans/month, full single-result breakdowns
• Pro ($14.99/mo or $99/yr): Unlimited scans, full history, priority processing

Subscriptions auto-renew through Google Play until cancelled. Cancel anytime in Play Store settings.

PRIVACY
We don't sell your data. We don't train third-party models on your analyses. Microphone access is used only while you're actively recording. Read our full privacy policy in-app or at credibilityradar.app/privacy.

QUESTIONS
support@credibilityradar.app
```
